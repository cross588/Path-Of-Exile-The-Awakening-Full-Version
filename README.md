## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Diverse Character Classes** — Choose from Templar, Marauder, Duelist, Ranger, Shadow, Witch, and Scion, each with unique abilities and customization options.
- **Dynamic Attribute System** — Each character combines Strength, Intelligence, and Skill, allowing for varied playstyles.
- **Two Game Leagues** — Experience the thrill of the Standard league where characters can die, or the Hardcore league where death means losing your progress.
- **Unique Currency System** — Trade items using orbs and scrolls instead of traditional gold or coins.
- **Extensive Gear Customization** — Equip your character with a variety of weapons, armor, gems, and jewels to enhance skills and abilities.
